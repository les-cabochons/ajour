# Sidebar

The sidebar shows what is on your plate on every page: the running entry, your active backlog items and the pull requests that need you.

<Shot name="sidebar" alt="The sidebar with the view switcher, the running entry and the backlog items" />

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> to show or hide it.

## Overview

From top to bottom, the sidebar has:

- **The view switcher**, to move between Time, Backlog, Pull requests and Projects.
- **Search**, **Filter by project**, and **+** for a new entry.
- **The running entry**, or **Start** when nothing runs. See [Running entries](/time/running-entries).
- **Your work list**: active backlog items, with pinned items first.
- **Pull requests** that need you, when a code host is connected.
- **Snoozed** and **Settled** sections, collapsed by default.
- **Settings** at the bottom.

## Switch views

Click the view name at the top to switch between **Time**, **Backlog**, **Pull requests** and **Projects**. Each shows a count: today's total, the number of backlog items, and the pull requests on your plate.

<Shot name="sidebar-view-switcher" alt="The view switcher menu with counts" />

## Search and filter

Press the search icon and type. Every word must appear in the item's title or its project's name or code. Case and accents do not matter. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to clear the search.

Press the filter icon to show one project's items only. Ajour remembers the filter on this device. Search and filter apply to the sidebar only, not to the Backlog page.

## Work with an item

Hover an item for **Start entry**, **Snooze…** and **Settle**. Click it to open its page. Right-click it, or focus it and press <kbd>.</kbd>, for every action. See [Backlog items](/backlog/items#actions).

A dot on an item means it came back on its own, from a snooze or a change in its source, and you have not opened it yet.

## Density and width

Choose **Comfortable** or **Compact** rows in **Settings › General › Sidebar**. Comfortable rows show the project, the title, the state and the remaining estimate. Compact rows show the title only.

Drag the sidebar's edge to resize it, between 200 and 400 pixels. Double-click the edge to reset it. Dragging it narrower than 200 pixels hides the sidebar.

## Smaller windows

Between 768 and 1100 pixels wide, the sidebar is hidden, and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>B</kbd> opens it over the page. Below 768 pixels, Ajour switches to the [narrow layout](/app/narrow-windows).
