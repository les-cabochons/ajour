# Submit your week

Submitting turns a week of entries into timesheet lines you can copy into your company's tool, such as Workday. Ajour does not connect to that tool. It prepares the lines, and remembers which entries you submitted.

## Open the page

Click the totals at the top right, such as `Today 3:47`, and press **Submit week**. This works on every page and at every window size.

## Review the week

The top of the page shows the week total against your target, with a progress bar. Ajour warns you about:

- Days under their capacity target, such as `Tue 6 has 6:30 of 8:00`.
- Entries with no task.

Fix these from the Day or Week view, then come back.

## Copy the lines

Each line has the project, the task, the hours and the comments. Click a value to copy it, then paste it in your timesheet tool.

- **Hours** copy as decimals, such as `5.00`, which most timesheet tools expect. The page shows them as clock time.
- **Comments** are the notes of the line's entries, joined with a vertical bar, such as `Standup | Code review`.

**Group by** changes how entries become lines:

| Option           | One line per             |
| ---------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Day and task** | task and day (default)   |
| **Day**          | day                      |
| **Task**         | task, for the whole week |

Use the day chips to drop or add a whole day, and the checkboxes to leave out a line.

## Mark as submitted

When the lines are in your timesheet tool, press **Mark N entries as submitted**. The entries get a check, and the Week view shows a **Submitted** badge.

If the week is under its target, Ajour asks first. You can turn this question off in **Settings › General › Under-target submit warning**.

## Change a submitted week

Editing a submitted entry marks it as not submitted. The Submit page then says how many entries changed since you submitted, and the button becomes **Mark changes as submitted**. Copy the changed lines again, then mark them.
