# Running entries

A running entry counts your time as you work. When you stop it, Ajour saves it as an entry on today.

<Shot name="timer-dock" alt="The running entry at the top of the sidebar" />

## Start an entry

There are several ways to start, depending on what you are about to work on:

- Press <kbd>S</kbd>, or **Start** in the sidebar, to start an empty entry. Its editor opens so you can pick the task.
- Press **Start entry** on a backlog item, or focus it and press <kbd>S</kbd>. The entry gets the item's project and task.
- Press <kbd>Shift</kbd> and the number of a [pinned task](/projects/tasks#pin-a-task).
- Press <kbd>C</kbd> to continue your most recent entry.

Only one entry runs at a time. Starting another one stops the running entry and saves it. The editor names this **Switch**.

## While it runs

The sidebar shows the running entry at the top: its title, the time with seconds, and the project code and task. If the entry is on a backlog item with an estimate, a bar shows how much of the estimate is used, such as `0.7h left` or `0.4h over`.

The window title shows the time too, such as `● 1:24 · Fix export rounding`, so you can see it from the taskbar or the window switcher.

Click the running entry to edit it in place. You can change its task, its note and the hours counted so far.

<Shot name="timer-dock-editor" alt="The running entry's editor" />

## Stop an entry

Press <kbd>S</kbd>, or the stop button next to the time. Ajour saves the entry on today.

To throw away the time counted since the entry last started, choose **Discard** in its actions menu, or press <kbd>Backspace</kbd> on its row.

## Continue an entry

**Continue** on one of today's entries starts it again and adds the new time to it. On an entry from another day, it starts a new entry today with the same task and note.
